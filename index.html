IRONSPIRE

A tower shooter. Hold the spire against the horde, one level at a time.

No energy. No timers. Nothing to buy with real money. Every coin comes from play, and you keep every coin even when the spire falls.

Settings

How to play
  • Touch or point anywhere on the field to aim. Enemies march down toward the spire at the bottom.
  • Stop them before they reach the spire. Anything that touches it takes a bite out of its health.
  • Shoot the falling crates to grab power-ups: Freeze, Rapid fire, Blast, Repair and Coins.
  • Kill streaks raise your coin bonus up to 1.5x. Coins buy permanent upgrades between levels.
  • A boss shows up every fifth level. It spawns minions and lobs orbs you can shoot down.
  • Keyboard: P or Esc pauses. Space fires in Hold mode.
